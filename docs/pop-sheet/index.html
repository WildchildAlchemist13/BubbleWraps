Pop Sheet

Endless bubble sheets to pop. Tap one, or swipe a whole row.

I can't hear the pops

Check that the chip at the top right says Sound on, and turn up your media volume (the side buttons while the app is open).

Some bubbles don't pop on the first press

That's on purpose. A few bubbles in every sheet are stubborn, like real ones. Press again.

How do I reset my counts?

Tap the "all time" line under the big number to open your stats, then tap Reset counts at the bottom.

Turn off the shine or the vibration

Open your stats (tap the "all time" line). Sound, Vibration and Tilt shine each have their own switch.
