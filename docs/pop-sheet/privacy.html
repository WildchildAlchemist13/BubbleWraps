Pop Sheet — Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026 · Published by Wildchild Alchemist

Pop Sheet is a simple bubble-popping app. It has no accounts and no sign-up, and we don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location. This policy covers the current version, which collects no data at all.

What stays on your phone

Your pop counts (today, all time, sheets cleared, best day) and your settings (sound, vibration, tilt shine) are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Tilt shine reads your phone's motion sensor to move the highlights on the bubbles. Those readings are used on the device, moment to moment, and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Ads and purchases

Version 1 of Pop Sheet has no ads and no in-app purchases. No advertising or purchase service runs in the app, and the app collects no data.

If a future update adds ads, they would be served by Google AdMob, non-personalized only and limited to content suitable for general audiences. Google would then receive device information such as your advertising identifier, IP address, device model and operating system, and ad interactions, and would handle it under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If a future update offers a one-time "Remove ads" purchase, the App Store or Google Play would handle payment and would never share your payment details with us, and RevenueCat would confirm and restore the purchase using an anonymous app user ID and the store receipt. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

We will update this policy, and the date at the top, before any such update ships.

Children

Pop Sheet is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The current version collects no data and shows no ads.

Your choices

  • Use "Reset counts" in the app's stats sheet, or delete the app, to erase everything stored on your phone.
  • If a future update shows ads, you would also be able to reset your advertising ID or limit ad tracking in your phone's settings, and to change your consent choice in the app's privacy options where offered.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
