Pop Sheet is a simple bubble-popping app. It has no accounts and no sign-up, and we don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

What stays on your phone

Your pop counts (today, all time, sheets cleared, best day) and your settings (sound, vibration, tilt shine) are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Tilt shine reads your phone's motion sensor to move the highlights on the bubbles. Those readings are used on the device, moment to moment, and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows an occasional full-screen ad between sheets. Ads are served by Google AdMob. We ask for non-personalized ads only, limited to content suitable for general audiences.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect device information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device model and operating system, and ad interactions. Google handles this information under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you're in a region where consent is required (such as the EEA, the UK or Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before any ads. On iPhone, the app only accesses your advertising identifier if you allow tracking when asked.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them; it receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt from the store. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Pop Sheet is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are non-personalized and limited to general-audience content.

Your choices

  • Buy "Remove ads" to stop ads entirely.
  • Reset your advertising ID or limit ad tracking in your phone's settings.
  • Change your consent choice in the app's privacy options where offered.
  • Use "Reset counts" in the app's stats sheet, or delete the app, to erase everything stored on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
